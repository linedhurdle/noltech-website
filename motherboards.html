What We Sell

NolTech carries a rotating inventory of refurbished desktop and server motherboards sourced from quality hardware. Every board is tested before listing — we only sell boards we can verify actually work.

Our motherboards are popular with PC builders looking to save money, repair technicians replacing faulty boards, and hobbyists building budget systems.

Our Testing Process

  • Full POST test completed
  • All RAM slots tested
  • All PCIe and expansion slots verified
  • USB, audio, and rear I/O ports tested
  • BIOS accessible and functional
  • Capacitors visually inspected for damage
  • Socket pins inspected and intact
  • Compatible CPU socket listed on every listing
